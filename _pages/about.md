I am an M.S. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by [Prof. Jinwook Seo](http://hcil.snu.ac.kr/people/jinwook-seo) in the [Human-Computer Interaction Lab](http://hcil.snu.ac.kr/). My research focuses on GUI agents and mobile UI understanding: how vision-language models perceive screens and predict the actions available on them, including interactions that are not visible on screen. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering and a B.B.A.E. (Interdisciplinary Major in Entrepreneurship) from Seoul National University in 2024. I also interned at the Language AI Development Group at Samsung Electronics.

I'm interested in **GUI Agents**, **Vision-Language Models**, and **Human-Computer Interaction**.

Feel free to contact me if you have any questions or want to discuss research collaboration.
