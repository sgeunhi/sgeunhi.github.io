Minkyu Kweon

M.S. Student

Seoul, Republic of Korea

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Jinwook Seo in the Human-Computer Interaction Lab. My research focuses on GUI agents and mobile UI understanding: how vision-language models perceive screens and predict the actions available on them, including interactions that are not visible on screen. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering and a B.B.A.E. (Interdisciplinary Major in Entrepreneurship) from Seoul National University in 2024. I also interned at the Language AI Development Group at Samsung Electronics.

I’m interested in GUI Agents, Vision-Language Models, and Human-Computer Interaction.

Feel free to contact me if you have any questions or want to discuss research collaboration.
